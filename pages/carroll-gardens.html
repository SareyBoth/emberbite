Carroll Gardens Flagship

Located on historic DeGraw Street beneath mature Brooklyn brownstone trees, our flagship kitchen is where EmberBite was founded. Watch our cooks smash fresh Angus patties on open griddles, fry buttermilk chicken to crisp golden crust, and blister pizzas in our 900° wood-fired hearth.

Transit & Arrival

  • Subway: F or G train to Carroll Street Station (4-minute walk south on Smith St).
  • Citi Bike: Station at DeGraw St & Court St (directly across the street).
  • Street Parking: Available along DeGraw St and Tompkins Park perimeter.
● Open for Dine-In & Pickup
