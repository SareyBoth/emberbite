The Story of EmberBite

High-heat smash burgers, 24-hour buttermilk fried chicken, and wood-fired sourdough pizzas made fresh daily.

Where It All Began

Two Chefs, Real Fire

Luca and Mia met working in Brooklyn kitchens. They set out to build a fast-casual dining room where smash burgers, fried chicken, and pizza are made with the same rigor as an evening dining table.

Their menu centers on high-heat flat tops, a 24-hour spiced buttermilk marinade, and an oak-fired hearth burning at 900° for blistered Neapolitan crusts.

How We Cook

High-Heat Flat-Top Smash

Fresh Angus patties smashed thin on hot cast griddles with sweet onions, locking in juices with crisp lacy edges.

24-Hour Shatter-Crunch Chicken

Whole chicken cuts steeped in seasoned buttermilk and double dredged for a golden, seasoned crust.

900° Hardwood Fire

Naturally leavened sourdough proofed 48 hours and charred in oak flames for spotted fast pizza slices.
