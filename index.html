Carroll Gardens, Brooklyn

Real Fire, Scratch Kitchen

EmberBite began on DeGraw Street with a straightforward standard: fast casual food should be cooked fresh from raw ingredients over open fire. Founders Luca and Mia brought their wood-fired cooking background to smash burgers, buttermilk crispy chicken, and blistered pizzas.

Every patty is smashed on screaming hot cast griddles, chicken is soaked in spiced buttermilk for 24 hours, and pizzas bake at 900° on hardwood oak embers.
